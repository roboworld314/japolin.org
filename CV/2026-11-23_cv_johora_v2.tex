\section{Research Projects}
\begin{numbered}[2.2em]
  \item[{[5]}] {\raggedright \textbf{CAAL-VLM: safety-aware adaptive logging for connected and
    autonomous vehicles} ([P11])\par}\nobreak
    \textit{Advisor: Sidi Lu (William \& Mary)}\par\nobreak
    \myrole\ Project Leader
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Logged camera frames by value: SigLIP measures redundancy, Qwen-VL
        flags safety-relevant events and semantic change, and a PPO policy keeps,
        downsizes, or drops each frame.
      \item Deduplicated LiDAR scans with BEV histograms and a dynamic
        Chi-square threshold tuned by a DQN agent from KISS-ICP odometry
        stability.
      \item Added context-aware sensor selection: Qwen-VL extracts weather and
        visibility, reliability scores rate each sensor, and GPT-4o decides which
        sensor to trust.
      \item Cut camera and LiDAR storage by 23\% and 27\% and mean latency from
        16.6\,ms to 9.5\,ms, kept 96.4\% of objects (vs.\ 70.5\% for heuristic
        logging), and raised rainy-weather mAP from 0.75 to 0.86.
    \end{itemize}
  \item[{[4]}] {\raggedright \textbf{Context-aware perception: VLM-augmented all-weather
    detection for autonomous driving} ([P9])\par}\nobreak
    \textit{Advisor: Sidi Lu (William \& Mary)}\par\nobreak
    \myrole\ Project Leader
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Studied how camera-based object detectors lose accuracy in rain, fog,
        and snow, when autonomous vehicles most need reliable perception.
      \item Used vision-language models to extract weather and visibility context
        from driving scenes and adapt object detection to it, improving
        all-weather perception.
    \end{itemize}

  \item[{[3]}] {\raggedright \textbf{BlindZoneEye: blind-zone detection, V2I-assisted mitigation,
    and event-aware recording for autonomous driving} ([U1])\par}\nobreak
    \textit{Advisor: Sidi Lu (William \& Mary)}\par\nobreak
    \myrole\ Project Leader
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Framed blind-zone safety at occluded intersections and crosswalks as a
        decision-before-visibility problem: the vehicle must act while the
        conflict region still looks empty to its cameras and LiDAR.
      \item Designed a sector-level blind-zone risk score that fuses LiDAR
        occupancy, raycasting line-of-sight reasoning, and YOLOv11 camera
        semantics, reaching 92.3\% detection accuracy at 41\,ms (versus 72.3\%
        for LiDAR-camera fusion).
      \item Built V2I-assisted mitigation, in which a roadside unit fuses its own
        sensing and broadcasts warnings while the vehicle replans with model
        predictive control (HD maps as fallback), reducing collisions by 76.2\%
        and near misses by 75.2\%.
      \item Developed an event-aware Black Box that commits synchronized pre-,
        during-, and post-event evidence, achieving 96.7\% event recall with
        58.2\% less storage than continuous logging; validated in CARLA with
        ROS~2 and on a scaled physical testbed (accuracy above 86.8\%).
    \end{itemize}
  \item[{[2]}] {\raggedright \textbf{SenseGuard: sensor-to-decision adaptation and learning for
    reliable agentic robots} ([U2])\par}\nobreak
    \textit{Advisor: Sidi Lu (William \& Mary)}\par\nobreak
    \myrole\ Project Leader
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Showed that across 20 detectors, full-rate pipelines trail a
        scene-current upper bound by 0.44 in present-world accuracy, as redundant
        and vibration-corrupted frames queue up.
      \item Combined SigLIP visual similarity, Qwen2.5-VL chain-of-thought scene
        reasoning, and a PPO policy that gives each camera frame bypass, reduced,
        or full fidelity with a safety override; reviewed borderline LiDAR scans
        with BEV histograms and the VLM.
      \item Added area-average and vibration-calibrated voxel-grid downsampling,
        and a confidence monitor that keeps failure episodes for targeted
        retraining.
      \item Raised camera mAP@50 on the RovaX robotic vehicle from 52.8\% to
        72.9\%, cut inference latency by 31\% and decision age from 136\,ms to
        63\,ms, and reduced LiDAR trajectory error by 75\%.
    \end{itemize}
  \item[{[1]}] {\raggedright \textbf{VibraBox: a vibration-adaptive Black Box for replay and
    learning from robot failures} ([U3])\par}\nobreak
    \textit{Advisor: Sidi Lu (William \& Mary)}\par\nobreak
    \myrole\ Project Leader
    \begin{itemize}
      \item Showed that a storage setting tuned on smooth floors breaks the
        replay-error bound on bumpy outdoor terrain, so safe data reduction must
        adapt to motion.
      \item Built a ROS~2 Black Box with a bounded circular buffer and
        trigger-to-commit persistence, combining BEV-histogram (Chi-square)
        frame deduplication, voxel-grid downsampling, and lossless LZ4/ZSTD
        compression in MCAP.
      \item Designed a DQN controller that reads vibration, KISS-ICP
        scan-matching stability, and write backlog to choose the threshold,
        voxel size, and codec for every LiDAR scan.
      \item Across 14 indoor and outdoor runs (32.34\,GB), used up to 46\% less
        storage, cut rotational replay error by up to 71.4\% and latency by
        51.7\%, and raised retrained-detector recall by up to 12.7\%.
    \end{itemize}
\end{numbered}
